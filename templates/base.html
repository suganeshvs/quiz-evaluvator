About AI Quiz Analyzer

AI Quiz Analyzer is a self-assessment educational platform inspired by Google Classroom. Teachers upload class materials (PDF/PPT) and students read documents and generate dynamic AI Quick Quizzes.

Workflow Guide
  1. Select Class: Open your enrolled classroom.
  2. Read Material: Open the Document Reader to view PDF/PPT content.
  3. Click QUICK QUIZ: Confirm your reading boundary.
  4. Take Quiz: Answer dynamic questions generated up to your reading progress.
